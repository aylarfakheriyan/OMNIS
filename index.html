Research

Designing AI as a mediator.

OMNIS is an early Research through Design (RtD) prototype investigating how an existing Large Language Model can be positioned as an interaction and mediation layer within culturally informed digital experiences.

Rather than developing a new AI model, the project explores the design possibilities of integrating an existing model into an interactive system and examining how conversational interaction can shape encounters with cultural content.

Design Principles

Human-centred cultural interaction.

Human Agency The system supports exploration rather than replacing human interpretation.
Cultural Context Interaction is constrained toward culturally relevant content and narratives.
Interpretive Openness Cultural encounters can support multiple ways of understanding and engaging with meaning.
AI Transparency AI is presented as a mediator within the experience, not as an authoritative cultural source.
